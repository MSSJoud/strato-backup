                        A.10 Real-data conditioning and the grouped Bologna formulation
                        The previous results establish existence and Bayesian optimality under the assumed model, but
                        they do not guarantee that every applied real-data discretization is numerically well-conditioned.
                        In the Bologna case, some columns of the full layered real deformation design can become
                        extremely small relative to the observed InSAR signal. The posterior may still exist, but the
                        inferred coefficients can become numerically unstable or physically uninformative.
                              This motivates the grouped applied real-data state

                        The rest of the Bayesian formulation then follows on a better-conditioned grouped state space.

                        applied parameterization. The grouped formulation is therefore interpreted as the practically
                        supported real-data estimator for Bologna, while the unconstrained full layered inversion remains
                        a theoretically meaningful but empirically unstable formulation in the current setting.

               C.6   Interpretation
               These diagnostics support the following conclusions.
                 1. The original real-data layered deformation-space inversion was genuinely ill-conditioned,
                    not merely inconvenient. Its raw stacked sensitivity operator had extreme column-scale
                    disparity, a very large condition number, and a loss of practical rank.
                 2. Grouping the state reduced the problem substantially, but the old grouped patch formula-
                    tion was still poorly scaled because the load-related response remained much smaller than
                    the groundwater-related response.
                                                          16
                        3. The current grouped balanced multisensor Stage 1 improved the problem in three main
                            ways:
                              (a) the operator condition number dropped by several orders of magnitude,
                             (b) the grouped state directions became fully ranked,
                              (c) the inter-column correlations became much smaller, so the grouped state directions
                                   are materially more separable.
                        4. The observability Gramian of the current grouped model is not perfectly isotropic, but it
                            is positive in all three grouped directions, showing that the balanced multisensor state is
                            dynamically observable over the assimilation window.
                         In summary, the conditioning diagnostics quantitatively support the practical shift from
                     an unconstrained real layered inversion to the current grouped balanced multisensor Stage 1
                     Bologna formulation.

              D.6 Interpretation
              The synthetic and well-validation results in this appendix support the following interpretation.
                 First, the synthetic validation block demonstrates that the inversion machinery is internally
              coherent and able to recover known signals in a model-consistent setting, as summarized in
              Table S4 and Ągure S1. Second, the external well-validation results in Tables S5 to S7 show
              that the grouped Bologna posterior is not merely reproducing InSAR internally, but is also
              consistent with independent groundwater behavior over a substantial fraction of the well network.
              Third, the trusted subset provides a conservative basis for interpretation and should therefore
              be preferred whenever the paper needs to make the strongest external-validation statement.
                 In summary, the current external evidence supports the grouped balanced multisensor
              Stage 1 model as the strongest real-data estimator in the Bologna application, while the earlier
              unconstrained full layered inversion remains unsupported and the current Stage 2 Swin reĄnement
              remains exploratory.
